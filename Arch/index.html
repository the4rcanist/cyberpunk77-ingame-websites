What is Arch?

When passion meets inspiration, an obsession is born. Arch Motorcycle is modernity at its essence. Since 2011 our aim is to provide our customers with an unrivaled cruising experience thanks to our innovative designs and engineering perfection. Every Arch motorcycle is custom-made to order and tailored to its owner's highest expectations. Arch Motorcycle is strength in moderation. We work tirelessly to find the simplest solutions to maximize our motorcycles' performance. We carefully select each and every component, from the sleek, practical body down to the axle. Arch Motorcycle is about fine tuning the little details. In the age of automated mass-production, we still believe that a motorcycle with soul can only be crafted by hand. All of our motorcycles are built from start-to-finish by a single engineer, guaranteeing the highest level of quality and precision for every Arch.

Arch Nazaré

The Arch Nazaré is a unique mix between a cruiser and a racing bike – like an outcast, it refuses strict labels. Equipped with a single-sided, aluminium swingarm and cutting-edge steering capabilities, the Nazaré is perfect for an aggressive riding style. The true meat of this motorcycle is in the engine, complete with a ventilation system designed by our very own engineers.
